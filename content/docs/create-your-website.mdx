## Basics of you page structure
Now that you have a pitch that other folks hopefully like, the natural next step is making a good landing for your YSWS.
Now there is not really a `boilerplate` on what you can add in the landing, thats lowk your choice but heres a few things you should add to attract more folks to your YSWS.

### "What is this" Section
A "What is this" section is just a explaining section where you tell what this YSWS is, its generally a pretty small section that tells your thinking behind the YSWS and give a small idea to the user on what they can do and what they can make.

### "How this works" Section
Now this is an important section and I recommend having this in your website because its a fundamental section that tells the user how you YSWS works and how they can `create projects -> submit projects -> send coins`

You can have diagrams, simple text, boxes anything you like here, it just have to explain it correctly and simply without over complicating it.

## There is no boilerplate

These are just some basics example you can look at to create your own website, don't think there is a PROPER way of making it, there is no `boilerplate` or a PROPER way of making your website, just make anything!! but please have `How this works`, `What is this` & `FAQ` those are basic standards that your website must have to explain some important things to users.

Everything else? Go wild. Make something weird. Make something beautiful. Make something that feels like your YSWS :D
