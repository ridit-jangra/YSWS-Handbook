## Heres how you start a ysws


1. get an idea (make sure its unique ... u can check [#ysws-drafts︇](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N) or [#ysws](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0710J7F4U9) to get inspiration and make sure you haven't js accidently copied other pitches or already made/over ysws).
2. make a neat pitch, explaining your idea without boring other folks and make sure its under the #ysws-drafts︇ message limit.. you can look at other pitches like mine (self glaze :D) eg: [wonders pitch](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N/p1789277926327439) [pixl pitch](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0710J7F4U9/p1787051105009089).
3. if other folks like your idea you can start working on the website! (even if other folks doesn't seem to reply or some other things you can still create the website for your own happyness :), if the website turns out well it might interest others more into your idea), you can look at other ysws websites to get inspiration and you can even use the [beest ysws template](https://github.com/EDRipper/ysws-template) made by mr beest himself for beginners... you can look at pixl or my other draft ysws for inspiration :D (self glaze again) [](http://wonders.hackclub.com/).
4. pitch again in [#ysws-drafts︇](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N) with the website this time, and wait for others to give their opinion and you might even get some criticism so be ready :).
5. you can post your ads in other channel like [#present](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C01GPEL6CGM) and wait for a sponsor to approach you (1 thing to note is that it can take weeks to months to get a sponsor but if your idea is really unique or a gappie js loves your idea then you can get lucky and get a sponsor in days, it took months for pixl to get a sponsor so don't give up and js work on your idea :3 and polish it).
6. real things start after getting a sponsor tho, the sponsor might do some meetings with you regarding the ysws and your goals and stuff like that so be confident and answer them confidently (you can make mistakes thats the process of learning new things)..
7. and if your lucky your sponsor will tell you that you can publicly announce your ysws as a sponsored one!!! after deciding a launch date (pls don't rush it, i learnt it the hard way) you can get posting perms for [#ysws](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0710J7F4U9) by asking your sponsor, your sponsor will give you perms!.
8. after that make a really nice announcement about your ysws and post it in [#ysws](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0710J7F4U9)!! make sure its not too long and make sure it describes your ysws really well.
9. now the main tasks. 1. reviews and 2. fulfillment, these are the main fundamentals of your ysws.... (make sure your per hour rate is decided by your sponsor cuz they handle the money part), if you haven't reviewed for any ysws before and you have zero exp with reviewing you can hire some free reviewers by dm'ing them or opening a reviewer form!! you can even post messages in [#hc-opportunities](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0AU38ZDLE5) but for some time dont' pay your reviewers, get to a decent budget first... after you get a decent budget you can start paying your reviewers .. i would suggest ~(21 cents - 70 cents) is a sweet spot per review.
10. fulfilling,,,, you can get hcb perms from your sponsor but if they say that they will handle the fulfillments than thats 1 thing of your back so be happy but if they do give you perms then its DOUBLE GOOD cuz spending money is SOOOO FUNNNN ... hcb is pretty simple and you will learn fast.
11. fulfilling physical items is a bit diff from fulfilling grants cuz its a bit more complex but more fun!!! you js go to a trusted website like ali express or amazon and place the order with the user address and user mobile number and pay with hcb and thats it!! you just share the tracking id with the user and thats it!!!.
12. if your review queue is fucked .... js hire more reviewers and once it stablize you can js kick the non-hard working reviewers .. :P

it contains mostly everything but if u have gen doubts and questions you can js post them in [#ask-the-ysws-orgs]() or you can js dm [me](https://hackclub.slack.com/team/U0ARC79GEAV) ill answer your questions if ik the answers obv
