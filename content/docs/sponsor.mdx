## Finding a Sponsor
Wooooo now its a really important step, without a sponsor your YSWS is just a draft which means folks can't ship projects yet.
Ok now the real question, how to get a sponsor? depends on your pitch and luck 💀, either your pitch should be hella good or you have a really good luck.... anyways, you have to send your pitch in [#ysws-drafts]() first and post ads in [#present](), gappies (sponsors) see those channels and sponsor the pitch they like the most and getting a sponsor is a long process, it can either take 2 days or 2 months :(, pixl itself took 2 months to get a sponsor and wonders took 2 weeks.

Andd if you can't find a sponsor, its been months, what to do then? either you can dump the idea or wait more, you can't really do anything in this case, you can't force a sponsor to sponsor your YSWS, you can either work on another idea or wait. It might sound harsh but thats the reality.

What can you do increase your chances to get a sponsor? work on your pitch, make it neat, explain everything in it in short like your pitch has to be less than 100 words, its the rule to post in [#ysws-drafts](), and folks tend to ignore long pitches anyways, i think we talked about how to create a good pitch in `Idea to Pitch` page anyways, second you can work on a landing page for your sponsor, we talked about it in `Create your website`, get more rsvp's & folks in your channel, we talked about in `Ads` and `RSVP` page.
A website and good number of rsvps and folks in your channel can increase the chance of getting a sponsor but i have noticed some YSWS get sponsored with no channel 💀 but wonders sponsor did ask for rsvp and i had around ~40 or something and they sponsored it lmfao.

Can u dm gappies to tell them about your idea? most gappies will ignore you 😭 and some might see it and yk you can get a sponsor that way but most gappies don't like you dm'ing them to sponsor your YSWS thats like the last step someone does before moving on from their idea.

Ik it might seem like its soo obvious things written here but thats the whole case 😭, its not a rule book its a handbook and i'm giving my experience and advice here i'm not giving you a set of instructions to follow and boom you have a sponsor, i'm giving and telling what happened to me and what you can do to increase your chances its not guaranteed that you will get a sponsor 🤷
