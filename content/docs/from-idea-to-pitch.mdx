## Finding an Idea
Finding an idea is the first step of creating a YSWS, your idea should be unique not boring. <br/>
Theres a catch tho teens from around the world have discovered a numerous amount of YSWS ideas, so if you're thinking of an idea it might already exists/existed. 
But lets stay positive and lets say your idea is truly unique and never been made before.

Ok we've talked about being creative but how to actually find an idea? <br/>
This is where most of your early time will be spent. Thinking of an idea can be difficult but its not impossible. If you genuinely can't think of anything, you can just go to [Programs](https://programs.hackclub.com) or [YSWS](https://ysws.hackclub.com), here you can find YSWS that are running or drafts (a draft YSWS is essentially just an idea and is waiting for a sponsor, sponsors will be covered in next sections). Or you can even go to some slack channels like [#ysws-drafts](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N) or [#ysws](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0710J7F4U9) to ~~copy~~ take inspiration for your own idea.

Your Idea can be niche or vague thats up to you, a niche YSWS is generally a small YSWS because of the category of project it accepts and vague (or YSWS that accepts all kinds of projects) YSWS are generally `Flagships` (Hackclub ran YSWS that accepts all types of project), or YSWS like [Macondo](https://macondo.hackclub.com) (it ended but it was genuinely a goated YSWS), some niche YSWS examples are [Go-Ship](https://goship.hackclub.com) or [Forge](https://forge.hackclub.com) (Forge only accepts Hardware project which is pretty vague but a cool example of niche).

## Writing your idea
I would recommend making a `Slack Canvas` (or any other canvas you like) to write them your ideas. <br/>
Like I did for my Wonders YSWS idea!

Essentially your Canvas should contain the name, the idea, the thought process behind your idea, a theme (like bananas 🍌) and a web design (if you have one).
Thats just a basic idea on what you can add, the choice is yours there are soooo much things you can add by exploring different programs.

## Writing a pitch
Its an important step towards creating your first YSWS, a good pitch attracts people and a bad pitch well we all learn from making mistakes so don't be afraid of writing a bad pitch, thats just a part of the process.

Now there is no ideal pitch, you can write whatever you want, you just wanna not make it boring thats it and keep it under the message limit in [#ysws-drafts](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N).

## Pitching a pitch
Now we have a pitch (its either good or bad, we don't really care), now we wanna send this pitch in [#ysws-drafts](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N) because its the main channel for sending YSWS idea pitches, now we wait for other folks opinions (they're real helpful but sometimes can be harsh so be ready).
Folks in *#ysws-draft* is real good and really helpful so you will get a lot of help there.


Thats it your idea is now a real pitch :) <br/>
Now the real adventure starts, this was just researching and finding the right place to have some *FUN*!!!
