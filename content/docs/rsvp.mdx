## What is RSVP?
A quick google search i did lmfao
> RSVP stands for the French phrase `répondez s'il vous plaît`, which translates to `please respond` or `respond if you please`.

### What It Means on Invitations
- Attendance Confirmation: When you see RSVP on an invitation, the host is asking you to let them know whether or not you will attend.

Sooo making a RSVP forms tells you how many folks are interested in your YSWS idea, and creating it is pretty simple..

## Why do we need it?
To know how many folks are interested and it helps you getting a sponsor cuz if a sponsor sees folks are interested in doing your YSWS then you chances of getting a sponsor might increase a little bit but it helps :)

## How to make it?
simple js go to its [website](https://rsvp.soon.it), login with your hackclub account, click on `New Form` on the top and enter all the details and you can manage your forms from the home page by clicking on your YSWS form!!! simple
